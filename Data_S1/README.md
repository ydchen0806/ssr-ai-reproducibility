# Data S1: computational figure source data

MANIFEST.csv lists every delivered file, its panel, original source location and SHA-256 checksum. Original paths identify provenance; the delivered relative paths are portable. DISPLAYED_SEEDS.csv and figure5_panel_sources.json map each panel to its evaluated cohort.

Figure 4A uses the September 30 collaborator artwork. C/D show cohort means and intervals without individual-seed symbols. H shows ten paired AlphaEdit orders as box plots of final-history efficacy and locality after 250 edits; six earlier efficacy checkpoints remain in the raw records but are not plotted. B/E report five paired seeds per dataset, C/D/F ten paired orders per comparison, G one full-recipe pair per setting, and I four geometry pairs per dataset. See figure4/COHORT_REGISTRY.md.

Figure 5 uses ten paired orders per comparison, all with 32-target rehearsal and complete-phrase matching. figure5_dual_followup supplies A, B, E, F and the rank-8 and rank-32 bars in C and D. figure5_rank1664_followup supplies the rank-16 bars in C and D. figure5_rehearsal is the earlier eight-target sweep; it has no history locality and is not shown in Figure 5. The rank-32 trajectory and geometry summaries are computed from its raw records.

Figure 4B--F and H use final-history efficacy and locality. G reports the recorded endpoints for each example. Figure 5A--D show immediate and history efficacy and locality with complete-phrase matching. C and D use the same 32-target cohorts. Figure 6 CUB/CIFAR AA and AF use final task-history scores; VOC reports stage-wise and final-stage segmentation scores.

Figure 4F displays seven arms. Table S2 uses the Figure 4B/E seed sets. Figure 6F displays three strengths, and the supplementary strength file contains all nine.
