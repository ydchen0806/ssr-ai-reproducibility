# Data S1: computational figure source data

MANIFEST.csv lists every delivered file, its panel, original source location and SHA-256 checksum. Original paths identify provenance; the delivered relative paths are portable. DISPLAYED_SEEDS.csv and figure5_panel_sources.json map each panel to its evaluated cohort.

Figure 4A uses the September 30 collaborator artwork. C/D show cohort means and intervals without individual-seed symbols. H shows ten paired AlphaEdit orders as box plots of final-history efficacy and locality after 250 edits; six earlier efficacy checkpoints remain in the raw records but are not plotted. B/E report five paired seeds per dataset, C/D/F ten paired orders per comparison, G one full-recipe pair per setting, and I four geometry pairs per dataset. See figure4/COHORT_REGISTRY.md.

Figure 5 uses ten paired orders per comparison. figure5_rehearsal supplies C at ranks 8, 16 and 32 under eight-target rehearsal. figure5_dual_followup supplies A, B, E, F at rank 32 and D at ranks 8 and 32 under 32-target rehearsal. figure5_rank1664_followup supplies D at rank 16. These are distinct fixed order sets. The rank-32 trajectory and geometry summaries are computed from its raw records.

Figure 4B--F and H use final-history efficacy and locality. G reports the recorded endpoints for each example. Figure 5A/B show both immediate and history efficacy and locality using complete-phrase matching. C reports immediate and final-history efficacy; its immediate locality uses substring matching. D reports immediate and final-history locality with complete-phrase matching across three rank cohorts. Figure 6 CUB/CIFAR AA and AF use final task-history scores; VOC reports stage-wise and final-stage segmentation scores.

Figure 4F displays seven arms; the eight-arm factorial source also contains Anchor+Spectral. Table S2 uses the Figure 4B/E seed sets. Figure 6F displays three strengths, and the supplementary strength file contains all nine.
